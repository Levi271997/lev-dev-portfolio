About

Design meets code

Six years of designing, building and editing, first at small startups and now independently with clients around the world.

I work in the space between design and engineering. I take a layout from Figma and ship it on the platform that fits the business, whether that is WordPress, Shopify, Webflow or a custom React build.

My focus is websites and web apps that load fast, meet accessibility standards and help a business grow. Every build is tuned for Core Web Vitals, because a page that loads in a second converts better than one that takes four.

Alongside the web work I edit video and design graphics for social, marketing and personal projects, with color grading, motion graphics and sound design.
